What we do

Nine services in three areas. For each one we prepare the full documentation and follow the case at the cadastre.

Parcels

  • Parcel subdivisionDividing a cadastral parcel into two or more, with the survey report for registration.
  • Forming an urban parcelCreating an urban-plan parcel from one or more cadastral parcels.
  • Boundary markingSetting out cadastral or urban-plan boundaries on site.

Buildings

  • Legalisation surveysSurvey and report for legalising an existing building.
  • Building survey and registrationAs-built survey and documents for entering a building in the property register.
  • Condominium plansDefining separate units, flats and shops, for registering unit ownership.

Surveys and property

  • Survey plans for designUp-to-date topographic surveys for architects and engineers.
  • Removing an encroachment noteDocuments for removing an encroachment note from the property sheet.
  • Property adviceReview of the property sheet and documents, with a plan for the procedure.
